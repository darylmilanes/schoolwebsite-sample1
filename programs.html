Enrollment Procedure

  1. Choose a Program — Review our programs and select the course that matches your goals.
  2. Prepare Documents — Gather transcripts, ID, and other supporting documents as listed below.
  3. Submit Application — Complete the online form or contact Admissions for guidance.
  4. Assessment & Enrollment — Attend required assessments/interviews and complete enrollment upon acceptance.

Application Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Official high school or college transcript
  • Government-issued ID (passport or national ID)
  • 2 passport-sized photos
  • Proof of residency or address
  • Additional program-specific requirements (if applicable)
